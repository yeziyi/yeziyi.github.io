Privacy Policy

Diwali Festival (the "Game", "we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected when you use the Game and how it is used.

Last updated: 2026-09-02

1. Data We Collect

The Game itself does not collect, store, or share any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts). Game progress is stored only locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising

The Game displays advertisements served by third-party advertising networks. These partners may collect certain data automatically to provide, personalize, and measure ads. Our advertising partners include, but are not limited to:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — Privacy Policy
  • Chartboost (Chartboost, Inc.) — Privacy Policy
  • Other third-party ad networks we may integrate from time to time.

These partners may collect:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / IDFA, IP address)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, installs)
  • Device and app performance data (e.g., crash and diagnostics data)

Data collected by these third parties is governed by their own privacy policies linked above. We do not control and are not responsible for their data practices.

3. In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases. Payment is processed securely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

4. Data Security

Data transmitted to third-party partners (e.g., for advertising) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is rated for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising shown to children, where applicable, complies with Google Play Families and applicable regulations.

6. Your Choices

  • You can reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • You can opt out of personalized ads via your device settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted at this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the support channel listed on the Google Play store page.
